HOW TO WIN THE REPLY
1. Their concern first. The first sentence after the greeting answers what the supplier cares about most (DOSSIER, SIGNALS, its last e-mail) - in its own terms, and with what our options do about it. Pick up one short phrase it used (no figures) so it knows it was heard.

3. Option A is the package. Name Option A as the package we can approve, and give the reason in one sentence drawn from the claims and the options. If no [[CLAIM_n]] is listed, the reason is only what Option A gives the supplier in its own terms - never call our price fair, justified, in line with anything or based on anything: a supplier answers such words by asking for the figures behind them. LATER E-MAILS
- Answer what the supplier last wrote. If it rejected an argument ("not our reality"), do not repeat it; move to the options.
- If it accepted an argument, thank it in a few words. If its tone is irritated, de-escalate.
- If it sent a final offer, be respectful and present our options as a serious effort to close.
- If it is waiting for an approval, make the options easy to take to the approver.
- If it asked us a question, answer it in words or say that the options answer it - never leave it hanging.
